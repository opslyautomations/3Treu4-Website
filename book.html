FAQ

Good to know

What kind of music do you play?

House and tech house are home base: dark grooves, rolling low end, heavy bounce. We also mix in techno, Latin house and minimal, and can go open format with hip-hop and R&B blends. Check out the Live Sandbar Mix to hear that side.

Where are you based, and do you travel?

We're based in Los Angeles and play regularly across LA and Orange County. For events further out, include the location in your inquiry and we'll work out the details.

How far ahead should I book?

The earlier the better, especially for weekends. Send an inquiry as soon as you have a date and we'll confirm availability.

Do you bring your own equipment?

Let us know what the venue already has (sound system, decks, booth) in the form, and we'll sort out the setup together when we reply.

Can we request songs or a specific vibe?

Absolutely. Tell us about your crowd and any must-plays or no-plays. We'll build the set around your night while keeping the energy where it needs to be.

Do you play B2B or with other DJs?

Yes. We've done B2B nights with DJ Jurassick at Woody's Wharf and shared lineups at District Orange. Let us know who else is on the bill.
